# Unit 01 — Introduction to Chemistry: Content Review

**Status:** Complete for learner testing; awaiting educator review.

## Curriculum alignment

The Federal Ministry of Education Inspire SS1 Introduction to Chemistry lesson lists these intended outcomes: define chemistry; list career prospects; explain applications; describe adverse effects of chemicals; and explain how scientists investigate using the scientific method. This Unit 01 package breaks those outcomes into four sequential lessons for easier learning.

## Important teaching decisions

- Chemistry careers are not interchangeable. Pharmacy, chemical engineering and medical laboratory science require distinct training routes and, where applicable, professional registration.
- Hazard means potential to cause harm; risk depends on the exposure circumstances and controls. Exposure routes include inhalation, ingestion, skin/eye contact and puncture.
- Never encourage learners to taste chemicals, smell chemicals directly, pipette by mouth, or mix household cleaners. Experiments must be authorised and supervised by a teacher.
- The worked scientific investigation uses dissolving sugar in water as a physical process, not a chemical reaction. Warm water should be teacher-approved; no boiling water or unsupervised heating.
- A hypothesis is supported or not supported by evidence; a single experiment does not permanently prove a hypothesis.
